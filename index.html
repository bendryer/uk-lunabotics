About the challenge

UK Lunabotics brings together teams to design, build and operate autonomous rover platforms across a set of challenges related to the construction of lunar habitats in a simulated lunar environment. Based on a US-based competition backed by NASA!

Challenge Objectives

Build an operational, autonomous rover. Navigate the lunar surface taking care of hazards and obstacles. Extract resources by collecting lunar regolith. Successfully return the rover to base.

Who can enter?

The challenge is designed for university and FE college students with support from their respective departments. Register today!

Why compete?

Gain hands-on engineering experience, backing from industry partners, and the chance to develop skills relevant to the ever growing space economy.

Thinking of Setting Up a Team?

Taking part in the UK Lunabotics competition is a great way to develop system engineering, design, test, communication skills, in a fun and supportive environment.

Teams from the US Lunabotics competition have set up a Discord for sharing knowledge, and welcome anyone looking to take part in the UK competition to join in the conversation.
